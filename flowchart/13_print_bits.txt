flowchart TD
    Start([Start])
    Start --> PrintLabel[Print BITS label]
    PrintLabel --> SetMax[Set maxbit = min of 100, 64]
    SetMax --> Init[Initialize i = maxbit-1]
    Init --> Loop{i >= 0?}
    Loop -->|Yes| PrintBit[Print bit at position i]
    PrintBit --> Dec[Decrement i]
    Dec --> Loop
    Loop -->|No| CheckMore{UNIVERSAL_SET_SIZE > 64?}
    CheckMore -->|Yes| PrintDots[Print ...]
    CheckMore -->|No| PrintClose
    PrintDots --> PrintClose[Print closing bracket]
    PrintClose --> End([End])
