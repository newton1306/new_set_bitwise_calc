flowchart TD
    Start([Start])
    Start --> Init[Initialize l=0, r=length-1]
    Init --> LoopLeft{Is s[l] whitespace?}
    LoopLeft -->|Yes| IncL[Increment l]
    IncL --> LoopLeft
    LoopLeft -->|No| LoopRight{Is s[r] whitespace AND r >= l?}
    LoopRight -->|Yes| DecR[Decrement r]
    DecR --> LoopRight
    LoopRight -->|No| CheckMove{l > 0?}
    CheckMove -->|Yes| Move[Move string from position l]
    CheckMove -->|No| SetNull
    Move --> SetNull[Set null terminator at r-l+1]
    SetNull --> End([End])
